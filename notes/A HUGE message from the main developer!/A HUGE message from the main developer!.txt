             Section 2.1: Kirby's Star Stacker (SNES, known as Kirby's Super Star Stacker unofficially, Kirby no Kirakira Kids in Japan)

             Welcome to Part 2 of this note. I will explain all of the games that inspired Puzzle Dreamer and its gameplay.
             The first section of Part 2 will go over Kirby's Star Stacker (the SNES/SFC version, not the Game Boy version) and how its
             mechanics became one of the main inspirations behind the gameplay of Puzzle Dreamer.

             Kirby's Star Stacker (SNES/SFC) is a spin off of the Kirby series, being a puzzle game with block dropping elements.
             There were two versions of the game that was released, Game Boy and Super Famicom. The Game Boy version came out first a full year before the Super Famicom version,
             and the Super Famicom version was only released in Japan, as its American counterpart, the Super Nintendo Entertainment System, was discontinued internationally.

             It was eventually released internationally on the SNES app on the Nintendo Switch Online service, so there's that.
